mossplanet ("this site") is a personal portfolio site run by the illustrator Moss (mossgray). This policy explains how this site handles personal information.

## 1. Information we collect

- Information you enter in the contact form (your name, email address, message, and so on)
- Information that the server records automatically (IP address, browser type, date and time of access, and so on)

## 2. How we use it

- To reply to your inquiries and commissions, and to communicate with you afterward
- To keep this site running safely and to prevent unauthorized access

## 3. External services

This site is hosted on a service provided by Netlify, Inc. (United States). Messages sent through the contact form and server logs are stored on Netlify's servers.

This site uses Google Search Console to see how it appears in search results. It only provides aggregated data about search results, and does not collect information about individual visitors.

This site does not use analytics tools or advertising cookies.

Some pages of this site embed YouTube videos. When a video is displayed, Google LLC (United States) may collect information about visitors through cookies and similar technologies. For how that information is handled, please see Google's privacy policy.

This site uses Google Fonts, provided by Google LLC (United States), to display text. When the fonts are loaded, information such as your IP address is sent to Google.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Messages sent through the contact form are deleted once one year has passed since our correspondence ended. Records of commissions and transactions (such as quotes and invoices) are kept for the period required by law. If you ask us to disclose, correct, or delete your personal information, we will confirm your request and respond promptly.

## 6. Contact

For questions about how your personal information is handled, please use the [contact form](/en/contact/#form) on this site.

## 7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised as needed. Any revised version takes effect when it is posted on this page.

This policy is a translation of the Japanese version. If there is any difference between the two, the Japanese version prevails.
